# Changelog

All notable changes to Starshot are recorded here. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and the project will use
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

Write new entries under `## [Unreleased]`. Release headings with dates and version
numbers are generated by CI release proposals, never by hand.

## [Unreleased]

### Changed

- Port the lunar flight to `starshot-physics`. On 14 TypeScript flights, a Moon impact among them, it takes the same steps to the same ending. Entry and end times agree within 5 ms but for one graze at 0.031 s, and last positions within 0.085 km.
- Port the lunar model, which reads the Moon rail by Hermite interpolation, to `starshot-ephemeris`. The five JPL probes hold within 0.1 km and 1.1e-5 km/s, and 72 relative states equal the TypeScript bit for bit.
- Add `starshot-ephem conformance`, which writes `fixtures/conformance/rails.json` for the Rust reader to match.
- Add `RailEphemeris`, which serves the planets, Earth's centre and the heliocentric Moon from the rails and falls back to Table 1 outside 1950 to 2150. Every body is within 150 km of Horizons (worst Mars, 65.9 km) and within its velocity budget, and Table 1 stays within its arcminute budgets of the rails every 5 days from 1950 to 2050.
- Add the DE440 rails reader to `starshot-ephemeris`. It refuses malformed or non-finite decade files and equals the evaluation in `tools/ephem` bit for bit at 2,719 points.
- Add Table 1, its Kepler solver and the `Ephemeris` trait to `starshot-ephemeris`. 303 of 320 planet states equal the TypeScript bit for bit, and the rest are within 6.8e-7 km and 1.5e-14 km/s; against Horizons the worst is Saturn at 8.614 of 15 arcmin.
- Add `fixtures/conformance/table1.json`, which `packages/ephemeris/test/conformance.test.ts` writes for the Rust port to match: 8 planets at 40 ETs, 20 ISO texts and 256 UTC times, among them each leap second and the millisecond before it.
- Add the `starshot-ephemeris` crate's time scales, vectors and bodies, ported from `@starshot/ephemeris`. Its leap-second table matches IERS Bulletin C at all 28 dates and at the millisecond before each, and its time conversions equal the TypeScript bit for bit.
- Move the Horizons fixture to `fixtures/horizons/horizons.json`, beside the conformance fixtures.
- Replace `tools/horizons` with `tools/ephem`, a uv-locked Python tool. It refreshes the Horizons fixture, which keeps its eight heliocentric states bit for bit and adds the geocentric Moon and Earth's centre, and it downloads NAIF's kernels against pinned MD5s.
- Add the DE440 rails pack in `data/ephem/de440s-100km/`: 20 decade files of 54,272 bytes for the eight planets and the geocentric Moon from 1950 to 2150. Checked against `de440s.bsp` every 5 days, at each decade edge and at the end, the largest error is 86.6 km (Mars) and the Moon's is 56.8 km, within the 150 km budget.
- Add the `rust-fmt`, `rust-clippy`, `rust-test` and `rust-licences` gates. Without cargo they skip and the last line counts the skips. `STARSHOT_REQUIRE_RUST=1` makes them fail, as CI's `checks` job does, and the new `rust-arm64` job runs the Rust tests on arm64. The size gate keeps crates out of `python/` and `third_party/` and on the workspace lints.
- Add the Rust workspace and its `starshot-math` crate, the only home of `sin`, `pow` and the other transcendental functions, over `libm` with its hardware paths turned off. Its tests compare 7,916 golden outputs bit for bit, and clippy rejects the `f64` and `f32` maths methods in every crate.
- Add the `commits`, `work` and `pr-size` gates. Commit authors, committers and messages go through the secrets scan, and each plan's issue file must agree with its lock. CI's new `pr-size` job warns above a group's size cap and fails above twice it, measured against the base branch's attributes and groups table. CONTRIBUTING.md now covers the board and status comments.
- Add the `dependency-licences`, `assets` and `credits` gates. Locked packages need an allowed licence or an exact exception. Every file under `apps/game/public/` needs a manifest entry with an open licence and its hash. The shipped observatory files stay checked against their own manifest, and the game assets section of CREDITS.md is generated from the manifest.
- Format the game and prototype stylesheets with Prettier and split them into ordered parts under 300 lines. Computed styles and screenshots match the previous build at 203 checkpoints in three contexts. Four rules that matched nothing are gone, and the prototype imports the shared sheets instead of copies. The size gate warns on lines over 160 characters in `.ts`, `.tsx`, `.mjs` and `.css` files.
- Moonshot's mission drawer adds Search routes, a bounded search of up to 240 flights, in a slot of its own. Its route changes nothing until Apply searched route, and an applied route caps the attempt at 2 stars.
- The Moon route search yields to previews on a channel message, not a zero-delay timer. A full search takes 1.4 to 1.8 s instead of 2.7 to 3.1 s in headless Chromium.
- On phones the map tools form one row under the top bar, with the Hangar as an icon. On phones up to 800 px tall the expanded deck puts its page tabs in its heading and packs Fine aim. Fine aim with the System view leaves the ring 191 px of free room at 390x664 and 167 at 360x640.
- In short landscape the result card and the mission drawer sit beside the flight deck, so Next mission and Apply guide take a pointer. The loading reason no longer grows the deck, and the waiting ring sits in the free room. A launch closes the mission drawer. Busy Guide and Search routes keep keyboard focus.
- Mission numbers and Next mission follow an explicit, frozen mission order.
- Add browser steps for the remaining Moon cases: restore and replay under WebGL, short-landscape outcomes and pilot switches, an offline Moon restore through the service worker, and the bytes a first visit fetches.
- Add `scripts/verify-lunar-loading.mjs`, which fails a build whose entry graph carries the Moon rail, which lacks a chunk it loads, or whose offline release omits a Moon or worker chunk. It reports raw and gzip bytes.
- Load the Moon rail only when a Moon level opens. Moonshot says "Loading the Moon." until it arrives and offers Retry if it fails; the entry script is 90,860 bytes smaller.
- A preview that failed can be asked for again. Moon controls wait, with a visible reason, until the Moon model is present; it still loads with the app.
- Add a Moon model loader that retries after a failure, and give the scene its Moon model on each frame. The rail still loads with the app.
- Build Moon launch requests without the Moon rail, and give the scene its Moon model from the view. The rail still loads with the app.
- Answer previews between Moon guide search flights, and report each guide's strategy, grid and flight count.
- Add a bounded, repeatable Moon guide search over 28 launch days.
- Test that captured launch requests stay with their pilot and edit, and add a defensive reset
  if one ever changes before its flight commits.
- Keep focused Moon positions in the same 3D frame as flight physics, with one visible Moon.
- Verify real Moon sphere-of-influence grazes between accepted flight samples.
- Split the campaign shell, controller, menu and deck into focused modules while
  preserving flight, camera and save behavior. Remove the unmounted legacy screen.
- Resume the V1 program from accepted orbital gameplay, with a current campaign
  cleanup spec, remaining lunar acceptance ledger and reconciled release operations.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-03

### Added

- Default ring releases to Plan and launch, with a per-pilot YOLO setting
  for launch on release. Keep the frozen shot available for inspection and fine aim.

- Moonshot in the campaign, with an active circular parking orbit and shared lunar worker physics.
- Craft, Orbit and System views, independent date and phase controls, and explicit arrival cues.
- Pilot migration preserving earned access and saved flights without awarding unearned playtest stars.

### Fixed

- Keep the paused craft, forecast and launch at the same exact time, including
  subsequent phase and fine-aim edits.
- Keep full orbits and paths visible, retain precise pulling on small screens,
  and identify impacts in forecasts, results and announcements.
- Preserve valid lunar departures when simulating edits from another device in
  the local Cloud demo.
- Publish the GitHub release after a verified promotion, even when its reused build
  causes the earlier build and registration jobs to be skipped.

### Changed

- Show the versions actually served by production and beta in the README badges.
- Add README status badges and a promotion shortcut with the production approval steps.
- Require the owner's approval for GitHub production deployments, without administrator bypass.
- Record Starshot's standalone GitHub status while preserving astlo history,
  attribution and selective upstream updates.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-02

- Keep the menu version short with Beta, Preview or Development when appropriate.
  About's expandable Build details retains the full compiled version for bug reports,
  including offline clients. Stable candidates and production show the same version.
- Add CI-generated semantic versions, a stable beta site and explicit production
  promotion of the same verified candidate bytes (D35). Main merge no longer deploys
  production. Protected tags, digest checks and immutable recovery assets bind each
  release; retries reuse its original successful build.
- Center the compiled game version in the menu footer, with View source on GitHub
  at left and About and credits at right. About shows the same version offline.
  Replace the extra top-left mark and tagline with A game by Kobe and the creator
  link; keep the profile control at top-right.
- Prepare the current game for Firebase Hosting (D34).
  One verified artifact, separate preview and production projects, keyless deploys,
  scoped offline releases, visible credits and clear local-profile copy.
  Merging the release work into PR0 automatically deploys a development preview
  to verify before the production merge to `main`.
  The remaining program resumes on a new trunk after the release stack lands.
- Use Firebase Hosting only for the release. Remove the Pages deployment under
  the owner's revised D34 choice; keep subpath checks for portable fork builds.
- Clarify Starshot's current game, repository links and verification instructions.
  First publication uses the verified PR0 artifact to check the production domain
  before the main merge. D35 adds explicit promotion for later versioned releases.
- Wait for resolved service-worker states in release verification, so an unfinished
  installation or missing waiting update cannot pass the browser check early.

### Added

- A main-game handoff for the approved prototype in PR0, with active-shell cleanup,
  shared architecture and Earth-to-Moon as the first mission in subsequent work.
- The approved 3D shell integrated into the main game: all four campaign missions,
  seven sandbox destinations, live entry, local pilots, local cloud-profile demo,
  mission deck, paged flight deck, inspection, hangar and result loop.
- Canonical campaign saves with original sandbox windows, hint penalties and
  generation-guarded restoration. Existing legacy star records remain intact.
- A shared presentation workspace for the collector, rear-ring engine, crew pod,
  drone, icons, audio and entry timing. Both apps use the canonical observatory media.
- Regression coverage for final-release identity, cancelled responses, restore
  eligibility, saved-window reconstruction, free-view aiming, ring clearance,
  entry continuity, close-up depth and replay speed stepping.
- A live orbital entry menu with same-scene Play and Continue, local profiles and a
  level picker. A labelled cloud-profile demo previews sign-in, restoration, offline
  drafts, version conflicts and sign-out without contacting Firebase.
- A true-scale parking-orbit reference and burn node, with travelled and future path
  styles. Radial-only vessel separation preserves its visible prograde direction.
- A rear-ring charging gesture with an anchored collector, a cool field chamber and
  release snap. The same engine can be charged in the hangar without changing flight.
- Shared Follow, System and Free cameras, a north-facing lunar orbit view and measured
  approach markers. The hosted Launch window preserves the current flight, clock and preferences.
- A compact collapsed flight deck with horizontal fuel and angle readings, followed
  by progress and Moon distance during flight.
- Direct result controls and a level restart. The collector faces its Earth-relative
  travel direction with a visual engine plume and unchanged physical impulse.
- A playable 3D Earth-to-Moon design study, with pull-back aiming, a shared worker
  preview and flight path, a zoomable camera and an original procedural vehicle workshop.
- An isolated Observatory prototype with the approved launch-window controls,
  local assets, source credits and reproducible astronomy-data checks.
- The Starshot game (`apps/game`): a slingshot across the real Solar System, built
  with Vite, React and Canvas 2D, playable on phones and desktops, with keyboard
  controls, screen-reader announcements and reduced-motion support.
- Four campaign levels on real launch windows (Mars in late 2026, Venus in 2028,
  Jupiter in 2028 and a Voyager-2-style Jupiter-to-Saturn Grand Tour from 1977), plus a
  sandbox that starts from today's date. Every campaign level has a known solution that
  the tests fly.
- Star scoring: one for reaching the target, one for an efficient launch (within 10%
  of par, 5% on the Grand Tour), one for a bullseye or the required flyby; hints cap a
  level at two stars.
- An aiming preview that runs the identical simulation as the real launch, in a Web
  Worker so the screen never freezes. The launch button waits until the preview of the
  aim on screen has finished, and the launch flies that preview's own request.
- A training level: First Light's capture zone is three times Mars's real sphere of
  influence, labelled as such in the HUD, the status line and the result, with a
  first-run coach mark ("Drag anywhere to pull the slingshot"). Every other level is
  real size.
- Deterministic hints: a search with a fixed number of simulations and no wall clock,
  rounded to the precision the controls show, so the hint's label is exactly what "Use
  hint" applies; when no exact hit exists, the hint says so.
- `@starshot/ephemeris`: planet positions and velocities from NASA/JPL's approximate
  Keplerian elements (1800 to 2050), time conversions (UTC to TDB), planet masses,
  radii and spheres of influence, and orbit paths for drawing. Tested against NASA/JPL
  Horizons at five dates from 1977 to 2049.
- `@starshot/physics`: an adaptive Dormand-Prince flight simulator with encounter,
  impact and capture detection, a patched-conic Earth departure, delta-v and C3
  helpers, a Lambert solver and a launch-window search.
- `tools/horizons/fetch_fixtures.py`: refreshes the NASA/JPL Horizons reference data
  used by the tests (Python standard library only).
- `tools/astlo-bench`: the scripts and results used to compare astlo with NASA/JPL.
- Automatic publishing to GitHub Pages and a CI workflow that runs every quality check
  on each pull request.
- Quality gates (`npm run gates`): secret and private-name scanning, a byte-for-byte
  check that `third_party/astlo` is untouched, agent-configuration checks, type
  checking, tests and the build.
- The agent working agreement (`AGENTS.md`) and its Claude Code setup: agent roster,
  review rubrics, skills and safety hooks.
- Documentation: README, licence and notices, contributing guide, security policy,
  decision log, architecture, game design, hosting guide, the astlo evaluation and the
  bootstrap plan.
- `python/astlo`: Mark Macharia's astlo v12 source, imported unmodified, followed by
  Starshot's fixes, one commit per topic: consistent J2000 orbital elements (Earth from
  24° off to about 0.2′), the Moon (116° off to about 1°), the RK4 step, barycentric
  velocities, sky positions (to within 0.06°), `ValueError` instead of `sys.exit`, a
  quiet import with an honest `Requires-Python >=3.12`, and a new looped RK4 N-body
  integrator. It is Mark's code, all rights reserved and not MIT, shared in good faith
  pending his permission (decision D10).
- `python/astlo/build_wheel.py`: a reproducible, standard-library build of the fixed
  wheel, `astlo-12.5.5130000+starshot.1`. The PEP 440 local label marks a patched
  downstream build (PyPI refuses such versions), and the wheel's description opens with
  one line saying it is a Starshot-patched build of Mark Macharia's astlo, not an
  official release. Plus its NASA/JPL benchmarks (`python/astlo/bench`), an offline
  test against committed Horizons fixtures, and `python/astlo/VERIFICATION.md`.
- An `astlo` CI workflow that builds the fixed wheel whenever `python/astlo` changes,
  prints its sha256, keeps it as the `astlo-fixed-wheel` artifact before anything is
  installed from PyPI, and then tests it.
- An optional `STARSHOT_PRIVATE_NAMES` repository secret (comma-separated) that CI and
  the Pages workflow pass to the `secrets` scan alone, before `npm ci`; forks run
  without it. Setup in `docs/hosting.md`.
- Decision D10 (the owner's call), which revises D2 for `python/astlo`.
- Licence records in the REUSE layout: `LICENSES/` with the Apache-2.0 and CC BY 4.0
  texts and two `LicenseRef` notices (Mark Macharia's astlo, and the Starshot brand),
  and `REUSE.toml`, which labels every path (D12).
- Governance files: `TRADEMARK.md`, `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` (Contributor Covenant 3.0),
  `GOVERNANCE.md`, `SUPPORT.md` and `CREDITS.md`. `CONTRIBUTING.md` now covers the
  DCO sign-off, issue claims and labels, the copy standard and AI-assisted
  contributions.
- Issue forms for bugs, features, level ideas and physics discrepancies; a label
  taxonomy in `.github/labels.yml`; the DCO app's configuration in `.github/dco.yml`;
  and a pull request template that names the issue and the AI tool used.
- The copy standard (`docs/copy-standard.md`, D27) and its `copy` gate
  (`scripts/check-copy-tells.mjs`). The gate reads Markdown, UI strings through the
  TypeScript parser, issue forms and commit messages. It fails on the em dash, the en
  dash and a short list of stock phrases, and warns on the rest.
- A `copy-lint` workflow that checks issue, comment and pull request text on GitHub and
  keeps one comment per thread up to date.
- A `size` gate (`scripts/check-file-size.mjs`, D28): no file over 500 code lines
  unless it is on a baseline that may only shrink, warnings for large components, and a
  layering check that keeps `packages/` from importing the game.
- A `licence` gate (`scripts/check-licence.mjs`) that fails when a file has no licence
  record, `LICENSE` is not Apache-2.0, a `package.json` names another licence, or an
  art or audio file is missing from `CREDITS.md`.
- `scripts/sync-work.mjs`: creates and updates a program's labels, milestone, issues and

  unless told to apply (D23). The `track-work` skill explains its use.
- The v1-starshot program plan in `docs/plans/v1-starshot/` (the plan with its 40

  and `docs/astlo/directions.md`, the technical note for Mark Macharia on where astlo
  could grow beside Starshot (D26, D29).

### Changed

- Starshot's own code and documentation are now licensed Apache-2.0 instead of MIT
  (D12). The four `package.json` files say so; Mark Macharia's files keep his terms.
- Game: result messages are statements ("Captured by Mars", "Hit."), with one
  exclamation mark left for a bullseye, and the hint tag no longer uses a dash.
- Documentation: ranges use "to" instead of the en dash, and a few stock phrases were
  rewritten, so the tree passes the copy gate.

- Mark Macharia's original astlo files moved, unmodified, into `third_party/astlo/`.
- The `secrets` gate skips its email rule, and only that rule, in Mark's own files under
  `python/astlo/` (his published contact addresses are upstream content).
- The Pages workflow no longer passes an unused `BASE_PATH`; its comment now says the
  game's relative `base` makes a path prefix unnecessary.
- Simulator settings are frozen per level and carried in each shot request, so a
  level's preview, launch, hint and tests still share one object (decision D6, clarified
  in the decision log). Sandbox targets beyond Mars use `OUTER_SIM_SETTINGS` and a
  shorter list of pulling planets, which keeps previews of 5 to 14 year flights fast.
- Pars are the cheapest shot found in each window. The Grand Tour's par is now the
  cheapest Jupiter-assisted hit found by a fine search, with a 5% efficiency tolerance,
  because 10% would allow nearly the full slingshot speed.
- The decision log notes that `python/astlo/astlo/**` and its `.dist-info` are outside
  the MIT licence (D3, following D10), and that D6's settings are frozen per level.
- The third-party guard and check messages now say that astlo fixes go into
  `python/astlo` as one-topic commits (D10), not to Mark privately as patches.
- Neutral wording ("a slingshot game in orbit") replaces a comparison with another
  game's name in the top-level README, `AGENTS.md` and `docs/`.
- CI: every workflow starts with no token permissions, and each job asks for what it
  needs. Every action is pinned to a full commit SHA. A new Scorecard workflow scores
  the supply chain, and Dependabot waits 7 days before it proposes a new release.
- CI: the required `gates` check now fails when any of four jobs fails, is cancelled or
  is skipped: the gate registry, zizmor on the workflows, dependency review, or the
  game's play-through in a headless browser. A failed gate shows as an annotation and in the job summary.
- The plan's specs now build as PR groups, two or three a session, with one bounded
  review per group and one master review of the whole stack at the end (D30).

### Fixed

- Game: the map and aiming preview render after development-mode effect cleanup.
  Cancelling a frame now clears its pending state so the next draw can start.
- `@starshot/physics`: `lambert()` now solves long transfers (more than 180°), with a
  numerically stable Stumpff C function and an upper bracket that steps back from the
  single-revolution limit until the flight time is finite.
- `@starshot/physics`: the departure from Earth is energy-consistent. When Earth's
  gravity switches on at twice its sphere of influence, the probe's Earth-relative
  speed is raised so that it still leaves with the v∞ the player chose.
- `@starshot/ephemeris`: `dateFromEt` rounds to the millisecond, so exact UTC midnights
  no longer print as 23:59:59.999.
- Game: a launch can no longer fly a different shot from the one previewed on screen.
- Game accessibility: the result dialog keeps keyboard focus inside it and fits
  landscape phones; labels are larger; every control's accessible name starts with
  its visible text.
- Game: the service worker deletes only Starshot's own caches, so other sites on the
  same GitHub Pages origin keep theirs.
