# Trademark policy

Starshot's code is open under Apache-2.0, and its original media under CC BY 4.0. The
name and the logo are not. This page says what you may do with them (D12, D20).

## What the marks are

"Starshot" and the Starshot logo and icon identify the official game and packages
published from this repository by Harmon Amakobe, who holds the rights in them. The
logo and icon files are in `apps/game/public/brand/` and `apps/game/public/icon.svg`.
They are all rights reserved; `LICENSES/LicenseRef-starshot-brand.txt` records that.

Section 6 of the Apache licence says the licence grants no permission to use the
licensor's trade names or marks. This policy is that permission, within the limits
below.

## Allowed without asking

- Saying that your fork, mod or project is "based on Starshot" or "a fork of Starshot".
- Redistributing the unmodified source code, with the name in it.
- Writing about, reviewing, streaming or teaching with the game, and using screenshots
  to do it.
- Naming the packages in your own package's description, for example "works with
  `@starshot/physics`".

## Needs written permission

- Publishing a build of the game, changed or not, in any app store or on any website
  under the name "Starshot" or with the logo or icon.
- Any name likely to be confused with Starshot, such as "Starshot Pro" or
  "Starshot Galaxy", for a game, an app, a package or a domain.
- Anything that suggests the project or its owner endorses you or your product.
- Merchandise that carries the name or the logo.

## Forks that ship

A fork is welcome. Before you distribute it as an app, or host it as a game for the
public, give it a different name and replace the logo and icon. Keep the credit line
"based on Starshot" if you like; that use is allowed above.

## The symbol

The official game shows "Starshot" with the TM symbol on its title screen and in store
listings. Others should not add the symbol to their own use of the name.

## Asking

Send requests to the project address on `playstarshot.com` once the domain is live.
Until then, open an issue titled "Trademark request" that describes the use, and the
owner will reply there or arrange a private channel.

This policy may change as the project grows. It is not legal advice, and it does not
limit what the law allows without permission, such as fair comment.
