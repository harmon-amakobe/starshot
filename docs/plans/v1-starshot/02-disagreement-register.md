# v1-starshot: disagreement register

Every place where two sources, a source and a decision, or a recommendation and the
owner disagreed during PR0's research, with the evidence on each side and the ruling.
An owner decision outranks a general best practice, and the ruling says so (the
`plan-pr0` skill, section 1). A decision that rests on a factual error is the one kind
the owner reopens.

Sources are the seven PR0 scout reports and the orchestrator's blind-spot pass,
summarised with their citations in [01-research.md](01-research.md). Repository claims
are `file:line` (re-locate by symbol if the line has moved), and external claims are URLs
the scouts fetched on 2026-11-01. Decisions are in [../../decisions.md](../../decisions.md).
